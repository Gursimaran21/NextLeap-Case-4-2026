## Case 4 : GameOn

**Designing a referral program to drive growth at GameOn**

GameOn, a popular casual gaming app, has gained a significant user base in the competitive mobile gaming industry. However, with increasing competition and a funding crunch, GameOn aims to focus on reducing costs and drive organic user acquisition and foster sustainable growth. The product team has found an opportunity to redesign the referral program for GameOn, focusing on key elements required to optimise user engagement, maximise conversions, and enhance organic growth.

GameOn's mobile app offers a wide range of casual games, appealing to a diverse audience of mobile gamers. With engaging gameplay, social features, and regular updates, GameOn has built a loyal user base. However, to sustain its growth and attract new users, GameOn recognizes the need to redesign its referral program and leverage the power of its existing user community to tap into their social networks.

As a product manager on the team, your objective is to redesign the referral program for GameOn in a way that aligns with the company's growth objectives and drives sustainable user acquisition. Specifically, the redesigned referral program should:

1. Motivate existing users to refer their friends and contacts to GameOn.

2. Encourage potential users to download and actively engage with the app.

3. Optimise program mechanics to ensure a seamless and user-friendly experience.

4. Maximise the acquisition of high-quality users.

5. Leverage social sharing to enhance program credibility and participation.

**Data Points:**

1. **Current User Base:**

   - GameOn has amassed over 20 million registered users worldwide.
   - The app is available on both iOS and Android platforms.
   - Users span a wide age range, with the majority falling between 18-34 years old.
  
2. **App Features:**

   - GameOn offers a diverse portfolio of casual games, including puzzle games, arcade classics, multiplayer competitions, and augmented reality experiences.
   - The app provides in-app purchases, virtual currencies, and power-ups to enhance the gaming experience.
   - GameOn offers both free and premium subscription options to cater to different user preferences.
  
3. **Market Competition:**

   - GameOn faces competition from other popular casual gaming apps such as Candy Crush, Among Us, Angry Birds, and Clash Royale.
   - The casual gaming market is expected to reach a value of $85.4 billion by 2025, with a growing user base across various demographics.
   - The average mobile gamer spends 21% of their total app time playing games.
  
4. **User Acquisition Cost:**

   - GameOn invests in various marketing channels, including online advertising, social media campaigns, influencer partnerships, and app store optimization.
   - The average cost to acquire a new user through paid marketing efforts is $2.50.
   - Referral-based user acquisition has proven to be a cost-effective strategy in the gaming industry.
  
5. **User Engagement Metrics:**

   - The average daily active user (DAU) for GameOn is approximately 1.5 million.
   - Users spend an average of 45 minutes per day playing games on the app.
   - The retention rate for GameOn is 35% after 30 days, indicating room for improvement in user engagement and retention.
  
6. **Monetization Strategies:**

   - GameOn generates revenue through in-app purchases, advertising partnerships, and premium subscriptions.
   - The average revenue per user (ARPU) for GameOn is $0.50.
  
7. **User Demographics:**

   - The majority of GameOn's user base is located in North America, followed by Europe and Asia.
   - Females make up 45% of GameOn's user base, highlighting the app's appeal to a diverse audience.
  
8. **Current Referral Conversion Rate:**

   - GameOn's existing referral program has a conversion rate of 2.4%.
   - This means that for every 100 referral invites sent by existing users, approximately 2.4 new users download and engage with the app.
  
9. **Existing Referral Incentives:**

   - GameOn's current referral program offers users a small amount of in-app virtual currency as a reward for successful referrals.
   - The reward is received by both the referrer and the referred user upon the referred user's registration and initial gameplay.

10. **Referral Participation Rate:**

    - Analysis of GameOn's referral program reveals that only 12% of active users actively participate in referring friends to the app.
    - The majority of users do not find sufficient motivation to participate in the referral program.
   
11. **Referral Program Tracking:**

    - GameOn has a basic tracking system in place to monitor the number of referrals and track the conversion rate.
    - However, it lacks advanced analytics to measure the impact of specific referral channels, track user behaviour post-referral, or optimise the program accordingly.

12. **User Feedback:**

    - User feedback indicates that the current referral program lacks excitement and fails to provide a compelling incentive for users to actively participate and refer their friends.
    - Users also express a desire for additional rewards or exclusive in-game content for successful referrals.
   
13. **Social Sharing Channels:**

    - The most commonly used social sharing channels among GameOn users include Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp.
    - Users primarily share their referral links via direct messages or by posting on their social media profiles.
   
14. **Existing Virality Coefficient:**

    - GameOn's current referral program has a virality coefficient of 1.1, indicating limited organic growth potential.
    - Each existing user brings in approximately 1.1 new users through referrals.
   
As a product manager on the team, try to answer the following questions:

1. What is “successful referrals” a function of? Can you write it as a set of product outcomes?

2. Which product outcomes would you focus on?

3. What are the potential opportunities to achieve the desired product outcomes?

4. What are the ideas you can think of to improve the referral program?
